Interaction Lab

Jiwan Lee

I am a postdoctoral researcher in Computer Science and Engineering at POSTECH, working under the supervision of Prof. Seungmoon Choi. I received my Ph.D. in CSE in 2025.

My research focuses on leveraging motion, haptics, and multisensory feedback to shape human experience in interactive technologies. My work combines psychophysics and human–computer interaction to develop perception-driven approaches for designing interactive systems and experiences.

I am currently extending my recent work and seeking postdoctoral opportunities that align with my expertise in haptics, multisensory perception, and immersive experience design.
